## Description
Run your cleaning business from one spreadsheet instead of a stack of sticky notes and half-finished invoices.

Built for cleaning and maid service owners who are tired of templates that only "sort of" work. Enter a client's details once and they flow through to every tab automatically, with no retyping the same name and address five times.

What's inside:
- Settings: your business name, rates and defaults, set once
- Clients: one master client list that feeds every other tab
- Jobs: schedule and track cleaning jobs, with dropdowns for client, service and status
- Invoices: filled in automatically from your Jobs and Clients
- Payments Log: who has paid and who is overdue
- Expenses: log business costs as you go
- Monthly P&L: automatic profit and loss by month
- Instructions: a plain-English guide to every section

How delivery works: instant download after purchase. A PDF with a "Make a Copy" link to your own editable Google Sheets version, plus a separate .xlsx file for Excel.
