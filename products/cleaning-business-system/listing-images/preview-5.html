Cleaning Business System
5 of 5 — What's Inside
7 tabs. Everything a cleaning business needs.
Works in Google Sheets and Excel. Comes pre-loaded with sample data so you can see it working before you clear it.

1Start Here

5-step setup, the color key, and how to clear the sample data when you're ready for your own.

2Settings

Your business name, sales tax rate, payment terms, P&L year, and a price list for up to 15 services.

3Clients

One row per client — contact info, address, usual service, custom rate, and auto-tracked balance owed.

4Jobs

Log every job by date and client. Mark it Completed and the price, tax, total and invoice number appear on their own.

5Invoices

Completed jobs become invoices automatically. Log payments and watch balances, status and days overdue update.

6Expenses

Track what you spend by category and month, feeding straight into your profit report.

7Monthly P&L

Revenue, tax collected, expenses and net profit, totalled automatically for every month of the year.
