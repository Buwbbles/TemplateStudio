How to start

  1. Sign in to a free Google account.
  2. Click Make my copy, then click Make a copy.
  3. Open the Start Here tab.
  4. Set your business details and prices on Settings.

What's inside

  • Start Here
  • Settings
  • Clients
  • Jobs
  • Invoices (includes the Payments Log)
  • Expenses
  • Monthly P&L

Color key

Teal headers = you type here  •  Gray = fills in automatically  •  Yellow = your settings
