# Template Studio — Buyer Reply Templates (T-008)

Status: DRAFT for CHECKER review and Commander standing approval. Nothing here is sent automatically.
Shop: Starnetup (Etsy). First product: Cleaning Business System (Google Sheets).

Rules every template follows:
- No discounts, coupons or freebies of any kind.
- No review requests tied to anything of value (FTC 2024 fake-review rule). Templates never ask for a review or a rating change.
- No promises we haven't verified (Excel support, refunds, no fixed reply-time promises).
- Fill every [PLACEHOLDER] before the draft goes to CHECKER. If a placeholder can't be filled honestly, don't send — flag it.

Placeholders used: [BUYER_NAME], [PRODUCT_NAME], [SHEET_LINK], [ORDER_NUMBER], [ISSUE_SUMMARY], [FIX_DETAILS], [SIGN_OFF] (default: "Template Studio").

## 1. How do I make my own copy?

**When to use:** Buyer opened the link and asks how to edit, save, or "make it mine," or says the sheet is view-only.

> Hi [BUYER_NAME],
>
> Thanks for your order! The link opens a view-only master so every buyer starts clean. To get your own editable copy:
>
> 1. Open the link from your download PDF: [SHEET_LINK]
> 2. Sign in to your Google account if asked.
> 3. Click **File > Make a copy**.
> 4. Name it and pick a folder in your Google Drive, then click **Make a copy**.
>
> Your copy is fully yours to edit. Changes you make won't affect anyone else, and you can make as many copies as you need.
>
> If any step doesn't match what you see, reply with a screenshot and I'll walk you through it.
>
> [SIGN_OFF]

## 2. "The link asks me to sign in" / "I don't have Google"

**When to use:** Buyer is blocked at a Google sign-in screen or says they don't have a Google account.

> Hi [BUYER_NAME],
>
> Sorry for the hassle. [PRODUCT_NAME] is a Google Sheets file, so it needs a Google account to copy and edit. The good news: a Google account is free, and you don't need Gmail as your email — you can sign up with the email you already use.
>
> 1. Go to accounts.google.com/signup and choose "Use your existing email" if you'd like.
> 2. Once signed in, open [SHEET_LINK] and click **File > Make a copy**.
>
> If you're already signed in and still see an error or "request access," please reply with a screenshot of the message so I can sort it out.
>
> [SIGN_OFF]

*Note for drafter:* If the buyer shows a "request access" or "you need permission" screen, the share setting on the master may be wrong — log a fix ticket immediately, it affects every buyer.

## 5. Refund request (digital file)

**When to use:** Buyer asks for a refund or money back.

> Hi [BUYER_NAME],
>
> Thanks for reaching out, and I'm sorry [PRODUCT_NAME] isn't working out so far. Our promise is that if something's wrong with the file, we fix it or make it right.
>
> Could you tell me a bit about what went wrong? For example: a formula or feature not working, trouble opening or copying it, or it not fitting what you need. If it's a problem with the file, I'll fix it quickly. Either way, I'll come back to you with how we'll make it right.
>
> [SIGN_OFF]

*Note for drafter:* This template does NOT grant or refuse a refund. Refunds, partial refunds, or any money-related remedy are a Commander decision — put the case at the top of the report with order number and the buyer's reason. Don't offer a discount, coupon, or a different free product as a remedy. If the buyer mentions a chargeback, Etsy case, legal action, or anything safety-related, escalate to the top immediately and don't reply until the Commander decides.
